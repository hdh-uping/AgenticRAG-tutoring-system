(3) 进队。

```c
void EnQueue(LinkQueue *q, Elemtype e)
{
    // 将元素 e 进队，即在表尾插入新的结点
    QueueNode *s;
    s = (QueueNode*)malloc(sizeof(QueueNode));
    s->data = e;
    s->next = NULL;
    q->rear->next = s;
    q->rear = s;
```
